# WORLDWORKS narrated demo (TRAILER-2)

Replaces TRAILER-1 (rated 2/10). Spec: `specs/TRAILER-2.md`. An edited product film cut to a narration in the user's own voice, not a playthrough. Nothing was uploaded or committed.

## Voice, music and sound

- **Voice:** the user's own voice, cloned in ElevenLabs ("emma-voice", model `eleven_v4`), recorded before this edit: `/opt/ww-video/trailer2/vo/01-hook.mp3` … `08-close.mp3` (first takes; the `-alt` takes were not used). The text is in `vo/script.md`. The picture is cut to the voice. Every phrase start was measured with ffmpeg `silencedetect`, and each shot illustrates the sentence being spoken. There is 0.45–0.55 s of air between segments.
- **Music:** "Astral Generated Music: 29c09952" ("cheerful game-like background music, bright and bouncy", 95 s). It was retrieved from HeyGen's music catalog through the user's signed-in HyperFrames account (Free plan) with the HyperFrames `media-use` engine. Licence: HeyGen's catalog terms for use in HyperFrames/HeyGen videos. The API returned no separate licence text, so check HeyGen's terms before any commercial use. The track is flat in loudness, so the build and the drop were made in the mix:
  - a low-pass (900 Hz) under the cold open, which opens on the splash;
  - a low-pass sweep down to 420 Hz with a dip from "So you drop in a Portal";
  - the filter snapping open with a bass impact on the beam (the drop);
  - a riser, then a hard cut to silence on the real receipts;
  - the bed returning on the standalone app and ending on the track's own outro under the end card.
- **Ducking:** a volume lane computed from the voiceover windows (−16.2 dB under the voice relative to the gaps, 0.15 s attack, 0.4 s release). On top of that, a HyperFrames voiceover carve (strength 0.8, against the `voiceover` group only) cuts 6 bands of 250 Hz–2.5 kHz and adds a level envelope with a −19.2 dB floor.
- **Sound effects:**
  - **The game's own synthesized sounds** (place, dial, coin, fail, pass, star, ship, beam, gem, tap; same oscillators and envelopes as `app/src/game/jobs/sound.ts`, rendered offline for TRAILER-1). They sit on the matching on-screen actions. Same licence as the repository.
  - **HyperFrames bundled library** (whoosh ×3 variants, click, pop, riser, bass impacts, sparkle). These are from Pixabay under the Pixabay Content License, which needs no attribution.
- **Measured, not heard.** Voice-only and music-only stems were rendered by HyperFrames from the same project (audio-only copies of `index.html`) and compared in 200 ms blocks where the voice speaks. Per section, the median music level is 17–32 dB under the voice, and at least 12.9 dB under it in 80% of voiced blocks; the quietest single block is 9.9 dB under. Effects (impacts, the riser, the beam) deliberately peak over the bed. After a first measurement, the long ones that overlap words were lowered. The final mix was normalised with two-pass `loudnorm` to −14 LUFS plus a −1.7 dB limiter (`scripts/loudnorm.sh`), with video copied.

## How the footage was captured

- Mock production build at HEAD: `cd app && VITE_CONTRACT_ID= npm run build`, served with `npx vite preview --port 4290` (localhost). `app/src` was not edited. Another agent was editing the title screen in parallel; `dist` was built before that and the title screen is not in the film.
- Playwright (`/opt/ww-shot/node_modules/playwright-core`, Chromium 1243, headless, SwiftShader GL). Scripts: `/opt/ww-video/trailer2/scripts/cap2.mjs` (shared) and `shot-job1.mjs`, `shot-job3.mjs`, `shot-gate.mjs`, `shot-factory.mjs`, `shot-shop.mjs`, `receipts.mjs`.
- **True 1080p at a real 30 fps:** every output frame is its own screenshot. `?step=1` with the existing `window.__ww2.advance` / `__wwFactory.advance` hooks, Playwright's paused fake clock advanced exactly 1/30 s per frame, and page CSS animations paused and stepped the same amount. There are no 15 fps holds. Dead time between shots (test runs, mock network waits) is stepped with coarse ticks of the same clock and never screenshotted.
- **Resolution:**
  - Job screens: 1280×720 viewport at device scale 1.5. That gives a 1920×1080 master and the game's own 3D canvas at pixel ratio 1.5, i.e. 1920×1080 backing pixels.
  - Factory: 1920×1080 viewport at device scale 1.5, a 2880×1620 master (canvas pixel ratio 1.5), so close crops keep detail.
  - Live shop: device scale 2, a 2560×1440 master.
  - Receipts: device scale 3.
  - Punch-ins deeper than the master's oversampling are upscaled. The deepest are the card quotes (about 2.7×, roughly 1.8× upscale) and the TESTNET badge (3×); see "Not done".
- **Framing:** at every mark the capture records the bounding boxes of the relevant `data-testid` elements, written to `cap/<shot>.json`. The edit list builds 16:9 crop rectangles from those boxes. Crops are clamped so they never slice the game's top bar, requirement pills or tray. A few gaps (requirement-row ends, the Portal-moment layout, the game's hint toast) were measured on captured frames and are commented as such in `edl.mjs`.
- **Saved progress** came from owner-local fixtures (`fixture()` in `cap2.mjs`, the same builds as the ui-tests). Job 1 was built from scratch with the tutorial dismissed. Jobs 3 and 4 started from a saved partial build (the Portal and the Gate missing) entered from the factory, so the drop is real gameplay. The cursor is drawn by the capture script and moved on eased paths.
- **Live site:** only viewed. The capture aborted any `/api/session`, `/api/customer`, `/api/relay` request and any RPC body with `broadcast_tx`/`send_tx`. The log (`cap/shop.json`) shows none were attempted (`blocked: []`). The cursor hovers the Pay button but never clicks it. No Ship, Pay, session or order was made on the live site. `/opt/ww-secrets` was not read.

## Self-review

Checked on `hyperframes check` (0 errors), on more than a dozen snapshot sheets while editing, and on 60+ frames extracted from the rendered files: every zoom, every caption and chip, both receipts, the moment, the end card, plus the 3-second contact sheet. Defects found and fixed:

- **Clipped UI at frame edges.** Crops sliced the game's top bar, requirement pills and tray, a sheet's `+` column, the shop heading, a receipt row and an explorer label. Fixes:
  - crops are clamped below the pills and above the tray, unless the tray is the subject;
  - the fix shot is right-anchored;
  - card quotes end in the gap after the third goal row;
  - receipt frames start in a row gap.
- **Overlaps.**
  - The chip covered the game's "Factory" button, Ada's job chip (★★★), a "Chi" label and the browser address bar. Chips now have a corner per shot.
  - Big keyword captions covered the tray and duplicated the sheet title. They moved to the top, and "Splitter" to the left.
  - The stars caption covered a pill, and the shop caption covered footer links. Both were moved.
  - The bottom-left URL chip covered "Status and method". It moved into the bottom strip.
  - The logo wordmark covered the Done, Test, Ship and shop-price UI. It is now a small mark, hidden in three short shots.
- **Wrong framing.** The Portal moment crop was far too tight (layout assumed, then re-measured). The fail shot showed the whole board with a tiny pill (now pushes onto the reason). The browser-frame camera math left a dark strip at the right edge.
- **Picture.**
  - The cold open began on the blocky factory overview; it now opens on editor close-ups.
  - The explainer built slowly with half the frame empty, and 0.5 s of empty navy opened it. It is now a hero title with larger centred boxes timed to the phrases.
  - The last value line had low contrast over the board; the shade is stronger.
- **Captions duplicating on-screen text** (Chi's card, the shop's sponsored-account line) were removed.
- **Capture problems.** Swap exhaustion stalled four parallel captures for about 50 minutes; they were re-run with a low-memory encoder, three at a time. Saved builds only load when a job is solved and entered from the factory; jobs 3 and 4 were re-shot that way. x264's default keyframe spacing made seeks land on wrong frames; all footage was re-encoded with a keyframe every 10 frames.
- **Facts.** The shop transaction hash was abbreviated wrongly ("…JjE"); it is now "…J1eE", checked against README.
- **Audio.** The first mix had music only 2–8 dB under the voice in places, and the riser clip was silent in the chosen window. Fixed by deeper ducking, no volume boost at the drop, lower impacts and the riser crest moved onto the cut. The carve first picked game effects as "voices"; the voice clips are now named explicitly.

## Not done / limits

- **Etherscan page not captured.** sepolia.etherscan.io blocks automated browsers with a Cloudflare check (TRAILER-1). The same Sepolia transaction is shown on Blockscout, a public explorer.
- **No live footage of the real Portal run exists.** The motion is a labelled, sped-up mock re-enactment, followed by the two real explorer receipts of the recorded job-3 run.
- **Some punch-ins are upscaled** beyond the master's oversampling: card quotes about 2.7×, the TESTNET badge 3×, cold-open close-ups about 2.3×. They are softer than native 1080p, though readable.
- **The factory is the game's simplest render.** It is used once in the cold open (last, wide), for the job labels and for the close.
- **The music licence was not independently verified.** It is a HeyGen catalog track; check HeyGen's terms before commercial use. The mix was measured, not listened to.
- **The 30 s cut's Sepolia highlight** is on screen for only about 0.6 s, because the cut has a 2 s slot.
- **The cursor hovers the live Pay button.** It never clicks (capture log `blocked: []`), and the chip says "Pay never pressed".
- **No separate ★★★ card.** Job 1 shows the ticked goals, then the ★★★ job chip as the game pulls out to the factory.
- **Fixtures, not a fresh playthrough.** Saved-progress fixtures were used for jobs 3 and 4 and for the factory.
- **Not uploaded, not committed.** README's demo-video placeholder is unchanged.
